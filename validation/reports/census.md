## How to read it

Each flight is classed by its largest Mach number: subsonic below 0.8, transonic from 0.8 to 1.2, supersonic above 1.2. The harness's flights are classed by RocketPy's largest Mach number, OpenRocket's by OpenRocket's, and the logged flights by hpr's, since a log has none.

A row's slack is 0.1% of its scale. The scale is the row's own tolerance where it has one, and otherwise the bar of its kind:

| row | scale |
|---|---|
| harness metric, held to a tolerance | that tolerance |
| harness metric, not scored | 3% of its reference |
| OpenRocket apogee or largest speed | 5% |
| OpenRocket stability margin | 0.5 calibres |
| logged apogee | 5% |
| logged climb (the trace's RMS, as a share of the apogee) | 3% |

For example, a 3% tolerance on a 1000 m apogee allows 30 m, and the census lets the difference move by 0.03 m before it fails.
